ATLAS ENTERPRISE AI — COMPANY POLICY HANDBOOK
Effective: November 2026
Version: 3.2

================================================
1. REFUND POLICY
================================================

1.1 General Refund Window
Enterprise customers may request a refund within 45 days of purchase.
Standard customers may request a refund within 14 days of purchase.

1.2 Refund Processing Time
Approved refunds are processed within 5 business days. The money will
appear in the customer's original payment method within 7-10 business
days after processing.

1.3 Non-Refundable Items
The following are not eligible for refunds:
  - Digital downloads after activation
  - Custom development work
  - Consultation fees
  - Extended support contracts beyond 30 days

1.4 How to Request a Refund
Customers must email billing@atlas-enterprise.ai with:
  - Order number
  - Reason for refund
  - Date of purchase

================================================
2. LEAVE POLICY
================================================

2.1 Annual Leave
Full-time employees accrue 1.5 days of paid time off (PTO) per month,
totaling 18 days per year. Part-time employees accrue PTO at a prorated
rate based on scheduled hours.

2.2 Sick Leave
Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per calendar year.
Unused sick leave does not carry over to the next year.

2.3 Parental Leave
Primary caregivers are eligible for 16 weeks of paid parental leave.
Secondary caregivers are eligible for 6 weeks of paid parental leave.
Both must be taken within 12 months of the birth or adoption.

2.4 Bereavement Leave
Employees receive up to 5 paid days for the death of an immediate family
member (spouse, child, parent, sibling, or grandparent).

2.5 Requesting Leave
All leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least
14 days in advance for planned absences. Emergency leave requires
notification to the manager within 24 hours.

================================================
3. REMOTE WORK POLICY
================================================

3.1 Eligibility
Employees in good standing may work remotely up to 4 days per week.
The 5th day must be in-office (typically Wednesday for team meetings).

3.2 Equipment
The company provides a laptop, monitor, keyboard, and mouse. Home
internet expenses up to $75 per month are reimbursable with receipts.

3.3 Security Requirements
Remote workers must use the company VPN for all work-related tasks.
Personal devices are not permitted for work data. Failed VPN logins
must be reported to IT within 24 hours.

================================================
4. IT SECURITY RULES
================================================

4.1 Password Requirements
Passwords must be at least 14 characters and include uppercase,
lowercase, numbers, and symbols. Passwords must be rotated every
90 days. Passwords must not be reused across services.

4.2 Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
MFA is mandatory for all company accounts, including email, VPN,
and internal systems. SMS-based MFA is not permitted; use authenticator
apps (Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, or hardware keys).

4.3 Data Classification
Data is classified into four levels:
  - Public: Can be shared openly
  - Internal: Employees only
  - Confidential: Need-to-know basis
  - Restricted: C-level and above only

4.4 Incident Reporting
Security incidents must be reported to security@atlas-enterprise.ai
within 1 hour of discovery. This includes suspected phishing, lost
devices, and unauthorized access attempts.

4.5 Prohibited Actions
Employees must NOT:
  - Share credentials with anyone, including IT
  - Use unapproved third-party tools for work data
  - Download files from untrusted sources
  - Click links in suspicious emails

================================================
5. EXPENSE POLICY
================================================

5.1 Business Travel
Domestic travel requires manager approval. International travel
requires VP approval. All travel must be booked through the approved
travel portal.

5.2 Daily Meal Allowance
Domestic travel: $75 per day. International travel: $125 per day.
Receipts required for amounts over $25.

5.3 Client Entertainment
Client dinners up to $200 per person are reimbursable with prior
approval from the direct manager. Receipts are mandatory.

5.4 Reimbursement Timeline
Expense reports must be filed within 30 days of the expense.
Reimbursements are processed within 10 business days of approval.

================================================
6. ONBOARDING PROCESS
================================================

6.1 Day 1
New employees complete HR paperwork, receive equipment, and meet
with their direct manager to review role expectations.

6.2 Week 1
Complete mandatory training modules on:
  - Company culture and values
  - Security awareness
  - Code of conduct
  - Benefits enrollment

6.3 Week 2-4
Shadow team members, contribute to a small "starter project," and
have weekly check-ins with the manager.

6.4 Month 2-3
Transition to full role responsibilities with a formal 90-day review.

================================================
7. PERFORMANCE REVIEWS
================================================

7.1 Review Cycle
Employees receive formal reviews twice per year: mid-year (July)
and annual (December).

7.2 Review Components
  - Goal achievement (50%)
  - Behavioral competencies (30%)
  - Peer feedback (20%)

7.3 Promotion Criteria
Promotions require 2 consecutive "Exceeds Expectations" ratings,
a business case approved by the VP, and confirmation that a role
exists at the next level.

================================================
8. CONDUCT AND ETHICS
================================================

8.1 Conflicts of Interest
Employees must disclose any outside business interests, family
relationships with vendors, or financial stakes in competitors
to their manager and the compliance team.

8.2 Gifts and Entertainment
Employees may accept gifts under $100 in value. Any gift over $100
must be declined or reported to compliance. Gifts from government
officials are never permitted.

8.3 Whistleblower Policy
Employees may report concerns anonymously at ethics@atlas-enterprise.ai.
Retaliation against whistleblowers is grounds for immediate termination.

================================================
9. TERMINATION POLICY
================================================

9.1 Notice Period
Employees must provide 2 weeks written notice for voluntary
resignation. Managers and above must provide 4 weeks notice.

9.2 Final Paycheck
Final paychecks, including accrued PTO, are paid within 30 days
of the employee's last day.

9.3 Return of Company Property
All company equipment must be returned within 5 business days
of the last working day. Failure to return items may result in
deduction from final paycheck.

9.4 Non-Compete
The non-compete agreement restricts employment with direct
competitors for 6 months after termination. This applies to
senior-level roles only.

================================================
10. BENEFITS
================================================

10.1 Health Insurance
The company covers 100% of employee health premiums and 50% of
dependent premiums. Enrollment opens during the first 30 days of
employment.

10.2 Dental and Vision
Dental and vision coverage is available at 70% company-paid for
employees and 30% for dependents.

10.3 Retirement
The company matches 401(k) contributions up to 5% of salary.
Vesting is immediate for employer contributions.

10.4 Professional Development
Each employee receives $2,500 per year for professional development
(courses, certifications, conferences). Pre-approval is required
for amounts over $500.

10.5 Wellness Stipend
Employees receive $100 per month for wellness activities (gym
membership, fitness classes, mental health apps).
